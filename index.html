What it does

Built around how you actually keep bees.

Not another spreadsheet. Not another app you'll abandon by July. A small, considered tool that makes you a better beekeeper.

01 / Catalog

A photo, a tap, and you've got a hive.

Snap a picture of your hive. Hive captures the location automatically. Name it, note the variety, and you've started a record that grows with every visit.

Whether you have one hive or thirty, every one of them has a home.

02 / Visit logs

Speak, snap, or scribble a note.

Sticky gloves and hot bees don't mix well with typing. Log inspections with voice notes, video, or a quick line of text — whatever's easiest in the moment.

Hive builds the timeline so you don't have to.

INSPECTION · 3 DAYS AGO

Added second brood box. Queen spotted, eggs present.

03 / Weekly report

A weekly briefing, just for your yard.

Every week, Hive looks at your visit logs, the local weather, what's blooming, and where your hives are in the season — and tells you what to think about next.

Not generic advice. Not a feed. A short, personal report you'll actually read.

⚠ NEEDS ATTENTION

Saffron — defensive behavior

Consider a frame-by-frame inspection within 5–7 days.

✿ OPPORTUNITY

Black locust is blooming

Make sure all hives have super space — nectar flow expected.

04 / Local context

Hive knows what's happening outside.

Weather, forage, bloom timing, swarm season. Hive folds it all into your hive's story so you know what your bees are walking into each week.

Who it's for

Whether it's your first hive or your fortieth.

Beginner Mode

Like having an experienced beekeeper in your pocket.

If you're new, Hive walks you through what to look for, what to do, and what each thing means. No jargon thrown at you. Just the next sensible step.

Pro Mode

Gets out of your way so you can get to work.

You already know what you're doing. Hive logs fast, tracks queens and treatments cleanly, and surfaces patterns across your yards. No hand-holding.

Why we built this

The bees are the point. The app is just the wax.

Beekeeping is one of the last things you do that's both ancient and quiet. We didn't want an app that buzzed at you. We wanted one that paid attention so you didn't have to.
